Her Sacred Journey

Life Milestones

Full Biography
Early Life1953–1981

A Child of Compassion

Born in Parayakadavu, Kerala into a humble fishing family. From age 9, she cared for her household while witnessing poverty among 60 homes daily — compassion was not taught; it arose naturally.

Foundation1981

The Ashram is Born

The Mata Amritanandamayi Math was officially registered. What began as a gathering of seekers around a young woman of extraordinary spiritual depth became a global institution.

Global Reach1987–Present

The World Opens Its Arms

First world tour in 1987 — Singapore, USA, Europe. Today she holds free public programs across Africa, Australia, Japan, the Americas, and beyond.

Awards & Legacy1989–2026

Recognition Without Seeking

From redirecting temple funds to care for 500 orphans in 1989 (the birth of Embracing the World), to chairing the C20 Summit in 2023 with 200,000 people from 74 nations — to receiving the inaugural Janani Award in memory of Dr APJ Abdul Kalam in 2026.

On Selfless Service

Four Pillars of Her Teaching

Vishwa Matrutvam

Universal Motherhood

Amma's core philosophy: every being deserves the unconditional love of a mother. Not sentiment — but active, tireless care for all of creation.

Seva

Selfless Service

Action without expectation of reward. True service dissolves the ego and reveals the divine in the one being served.

Ahimsa

Non-Violence

Non-violence in thought, word, and deed — extending compassion not just to humans but to all sentient beings and the natural world.

Dhyana

Inner Stillness

Meditation as the path to realizing our true nature. The mind quieted is the mind that hears the voice of the heart.
